## Meeting flow

Auth User

1.  Login
2.  Create a meeting (instant/schedule) for (authenticated/unauthentucated users)
3.  Meeting can be shared via
    a. Invite mail
    b. link
4.  Host can make the meeting adjustable by
    a. Time
    b. Open/shchedule
    c. authunauth users
    d. ends/cancel
    e. Can make co-host(s)

## Invited Users (Authenticated/uanthenticate)

1. Either Login/not depends on the current user
2. Joins the meeting

## Meeting

Duriong the meeting users can

1. Mute/Unmute their only state except host(s)
2. Toggle video
3. Leave/Join again

## Pre-meeting room

Users can

1. Preview themselves with audio and video
2. Adjust their bacgorund video
3. Adjust their color
